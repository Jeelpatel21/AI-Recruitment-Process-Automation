Pipeline Walkthrough
📥
Application Intake & Eligibility Check
Stage 1

A candidate submits a job application form (via Google Forms / webhook). The system immediately fetches the latest record from Google Sheets, extracts the candidate's details (name, email, phone, job role, device availability), and identifies the matching role requirements from a separate role configuration sheet.

An AI Agent (powered by Google Gemini) then compares the candidate's profile against mandatory requirements for the role — such as whether a laptop, smartphone, or internet connection is required. If any mandatory condition is unmet, a professional rejection email is auto-sent and the pipeline terminates for that candidate.

Webhook trigger Google Sheets lookup Gemini AI eligibility check Auto-reject if ineligible
🤖
AI Resume Analysis & Scoring
Stage 2

If the candidate passes eligibility, their resume PDF is downloaded from Google Drive and parsed by three parallel AI components:

Qualifications Extractor — summarises education history, job experience, and technical skills in bullet form. Personal Data Extractor — pulls phone, city, and birthdate. HR Expert Scorer — receives the candidate summary alongside the job profile description, then issues a numeric vote (1–10) and written consideration explaining the score.

All extracted data and the AI score are appended to a "Resume Analysis" tab in Google Sheets for HR review, and an HR confirmation email is sent with Approve / Reject buttons embedded.

PDF extraction AI qualification extraction AI 1–10 HR scoring Google Sheets logging HR approval email
📅
Assessment Round
Stage 3

If HR approves the resume, the candidate receives an email with a Google Calendar booking link to schedule their Assessment Round. Once the booking is detected (Google Calendar trigger), the system validates the Candidate ID embedded in the calendar event description.

If the ID is missing, a clarification email is sent and the invalid calendar event is deleted. If valid, both the candidate and the assigned HR receive confirmation emails with meeting details (date, time, Google Meet link). After the interview, the system emails the HR a feedback form (Approved / Declined / Absent dropdown) and waits for the response. The decision is recorded in Google Sheets and the candidate is notified accordingly. If absent, a rebook link is offered.

Google Calendar trigger Candidate ID validation HR feedback form Approve → advance to HR Round Decline / Absent → email sent
👤
HR Round
Stage 4

Candidates who clear the Assessment Round receive a booking link for the HR Round. The exact same validated scheduling flow from Stage 3 repeats — calendar booking detection, Candidate ID check, confirmation emails to both parties, and post-interview HR feedback collection.

Upon HR approval, the candidate is directed to book the final Domain Round. Declines and absences trigger appropriate emails, and Google Sheets is updated at every decision point.

Google Calendar trigger Same flow as Assessment Approve → advance to Domain Round Decline / Absent → email sent
🧠
Domain (Technical) Round
Stage 5

The Domain Round follows the same automated scheduling and feedback loop. After approval, the system determines whether the selected role is an Internship or a direct Employee position, and branches accordingly.

For interns: a welcome email is sent, the candidate is marked as Selected in Google Sheets, a joining date is calculated (day after interview), joining details are emailed, an Offer Letter is auto-generated in HTML, converted to PDF, and emailed with the actual joining date. HR separately confirms the actual joining via an interactive email form.

Approved → offer letter generated HTML → PDF conversion Joining date calculation Decline / Absent → email sent
🎓
Internship Lifecycle & Progression
Stage 6

After an intern joins, the system waits 3 months (using a time-based Wait node). At completion, an internship-end email is sent requesting a task report submission, and the Google Sheets record is updated to "Internship Completed: Yes."

HR is then asked via an automated email whether to advance the intern to Traineeship. If yes, the candidate is notified of the traineeship selection; if no, a professional decline is sent. After the traineeship period, HR is asked again whether to promote to Employee. Promotion or decline emails are dispatched automatically, and the record updated throughout.

3-month wait node Internship → Traineeship decision Traineeship → Employee decision Full lifecycle tracking in Sheets
